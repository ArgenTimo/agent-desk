What is covered, and what is not

Both columns are written by the same people and shown at the same size. If something is missing from the right-hand column, it is missing because it is covered — not because it did not fit.

Car

Excess from £250

Covered

  • Damage to your car and to somebody else's
  • Fire and theft, including the keys
  • Windscreen, without affecting your no-claims
  • A courtesy car while yours is repaired
  • Driving in the EU for up to 90 days

Not covered

  • Wear, tear and mechanical failure
  • Anyone driving who is not on the policy
  • Use for hire, reward or delivery work
  • Belongings left in the car overnight
  • Track days and competitive driving

Home

Excess from £350

Covered

  • Fire, flood, storm and escape of water
  • Theft and attempted theft
  • Alternative accommodation while it is repaired
  • Liability to visitors
  • Contents away from home, if you add it

Not covered

  • Damage that happened gradually
  • A home left empty for more than 60 days
  • Anything used for a business at the address
  • Subsidence, unless you add it
  • Damage by a tenant or a lodger

Travel

Excess from £100

Covered

  • Medical treatment and getting you home
  • Cancellation for a reason on the list
  • Baggage and delayed baggage
  • Missed departure outside your control
  • Legal costs abroad

Not covered

  • A condition you did not declare
  • Travel against official advice

Questions people actually ask

Is the estimate on this page the price I will pay?

No. It is worked out from three answers, and a real quote uses more — where the car is kept, who else drives it, what has been claimed before. The estimate is honest about the direction it moves in: more value up, more excess down.

What happens at renewal?

We email you 21 days before, showing last year's price next to this year's. If you do nothing the policy renews; if you cancel before it starts you pay nothing.

Can I cancel?

Within 14 days, in full, if you have not claimed. After that you pay for the cover you have used and we return the rest.

Does making a claim put the price up?

Usually yes, and for most claims for several years. A windscreen claim on a car policy does not affect your no-claims discount.

What do you do with what I type here?

The estimator sends nothing anywhere — it does the arithmetic in your browser. Anything you enter on a real quote is used to price and issue the policy, and the privacy notice says who else sees it.
